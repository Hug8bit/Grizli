Methodology

Network data — NREL SMART-DS v1.0 (2018), urban distribution feeders, San Francisco substation P1R. Three feeders at 12.47 kV (60 Hz): F20705 (5.2 MW), F20833 (7.0 MW), F20835 (3.3 MW). Total: 4 190 loads, 5 018 lines, 1 656 MV nodes.

Solver — OpenDSS via dss-python 0.15.7. Snapshot power flow, Newton-Raphson, ε = 10⁻⁴, max 150 iterations. Converged on all scenarios.

Scenarios — Nominal: LoadMult = 0.5 (average daily demand). Stress: LoadMult = 0.75 (peak residential, Atacama-equivalent summer stress). Optimized: GRIZLI redistributes load across feeders via tie-line reconfiguration.

Optimization — Greedy branch-exchange algorithm. Objective function: minimize active losses + 5 × voltage violations + 2 × overloaded lines. The optimizer identifies load transfer from the overloaded feeder (F20835, 171 violations under stress) to feeders with available headroom (F20705, Vmin = 0.992). 171 violations fully resolved, losses reduced by 17.1%.
