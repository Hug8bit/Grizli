Technical Study — Distribution Network Optimization

Topology Optimization of a Medium-Voltage
Radial Feeder under High PV Penetration

Región de Antofagasta — Chile

This document presents the results of an AC power flow analysis performed on a 33-bus, 13.2 kV radial distribution network representative of the Atacama region. Two operating scenarios are examined: evening peak demand under a load growth hypothesis consistent with the regional mining sector expansion (+30%, 2020–2024), and midday maximum PV injection under measured Atacama irradiance conditions (GHI = 950 W/m²). In both cases, a greedy topology reconfiguration algorithm is applied and benchmarked against the default radial configuration. All power flow calculations are performed with PandaPower 3.4.0 (Newton-Raphson method, convergence threshold 10⁻⁸ MVA).

Network model: IEEE 33-bus — adapted to 13.2 kV / 50 Hz (CNE Chile) · PV capacity: 1,500 kWp across 3 injection points · Solver: PandaPower 3.4.0 — April 2026

Executive Summary
Under evening peak stress conditions (+30% load growth, representative of Atacama mining expansion 2020–2024), Grizli's topology optimizer reduces network losses by 40.5% (from 320 kW to 190.5 kW) and eliminates 19 of 21 under-voltage violations in 4 switching operations computed in under 4 seconds — without any hardware investment.

Problem Context
The Atacama Challenge: Chile's Atacama Desert has the world's highest solar irradiance (GHI 2,500–3,000 kWh/m²/yr, DNI up to 3,500 kWh/m²/yr). Distribution networks in this region, originally designed for unidirectional power flow, are increasingly stressed by massive PV injection, rapid mining sector load growth, and long radial feeders — the exact conditions where topology optimization delivers the most value.
